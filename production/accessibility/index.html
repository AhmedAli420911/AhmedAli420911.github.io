Our approach

Service Capture Co. aims to make information and the System Review request usable by people with different access needs. This website uses semantic headings, visible keyboard focus, labelled form fields, text explanations for errors, expandable questions and layouts that adapt to screen size. Motion is reduced when your device requests it.

Using the website

Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move between links and controls, Enter to follow links, and Enter or Space to operate buttons and FAQ questions. The first keyboard link skips navigation. On small screens, the navigation button opens the menu; Escape closes the open menu. Browser zoom and text resizing are supported.

Demonstration and external services

The EverWarm demonstration opens in a separate tab. It includes its own navigation and form controls. If a future calendar or other external service presents a barrier, an accessible alternative will be offered through the contact methods below.

Feedback and assistance

If you encounter a barrier, please describe the page, what you were trying to do, and the browser or assistive technology involved. Include only details you are comfortable sharing. Use the contact methods below or the System Review form.

Ongoing review

Accessibility checks were included in development, but this statement does not claim certification or complete conformance with a standard. Reports of barriers help us find and fix problems.
