Privacy

Pre-launch draft · Professional review required

This page must be reviewed and updated before public launch to reflect the actual business, service providers and operating practices. It does not guarantee compliance with any law or standard.

Information in a System Review request

The contact form asks for your full name, business email, company name and the inquiry-handling problem you describe. Details such as your website, phone number, service region, inquiry volume and current software can be discussed during the System Review; they are not collected by this initial form. Your agreement to be contacted is included with the request. Please do not include customer records, passwords or sensitive personal information.

Why information is collected and used

Once a submission service is connected, these details are used to assess whether the system fits your business, respond to your request and arrange the System Review. Contact consent for this request is not a subscription to marketing messages. The packaged preview has no connected submission service and does not transmit or save form entries.

Service providers

A selected hosting provider may process technical connection information such as IP addresses and request logs. Once configured, the form, email, calendar or CRM providers may process request information to deliver those services. The actual providers, processing locations and applicable terms must be identified before launch. No analytics service is connected in this package. Do not activate analytics until the provider and consent approach have been reviewed.

Retention and deletion

Request information should be retained only as long as needed to handle the inquiry and any agreed services, subject to applicable recordkeeping obligations. Before launch, Service Capture Co. must set and document a retention period, access controls, deletion procedure and any backup retention limits for the selected providers. This draft does not claim those procedures are already operating.

Access, correction and deletion requests

Once business contact details are configured, use the contact method below to request access, correction or deletion of information you supplied. We may need to verify that the request concerns your information. Any limits required by applicable obligations should be explained when responding. Until a contact method and the form are connected, this preview cannot receive privacy requests.

The EverWarm demonstration

EverWarm Home Comfort is fictional. Enter fictional information only. Its example inquiries are stored locally in your browser; when storage is unavailable, the demo uses temporary memory. It does not send real calls, messages, bookings or CRM actions. Use its reset control to restore fictional sample data, or clear the demo’s browser storage to remove all local records. Storage is specific to your browser and the site or file from which the demo is opened.
