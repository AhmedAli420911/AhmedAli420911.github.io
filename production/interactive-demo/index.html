INSIDE EVERWARM HOME COMFORT

Explore both sides of the workflow.

The homeowner experience and the staff view are part of the same fictional demonstration.

01

Website estimate form

Enter an invented name and contact details in the homeowner estimate form. Follow the confirmation to see the captured inquiry.

02

After-hours call simulation

From the business workflow, choose Simulate After-Hours Call. Advance a scripted conversation and inspect its summary and follow-up task.

03

Missed-call recovery

Choose missed-call recovery in the business workflow. Complete a fictional scenario and see how the inquiry is categorized for human follow-up.

04

Unified inquiry tracker

Search and filter requests, open an inquiry, edit the next action and update its callback status. Manual office entry is available too.

A demonstration, with clear limits.

EverWarm is not a real customer, case study or business result. Its example records stay in your browser. No real calls, messages, notifications, bookings, dispatch or CRM actions occur. Use Reset Demo Data in the tracker to restore the fictional examples. Clearing the demo’s browser storage removes your local records.
